Key differentiators at a glance

  • X-47 Only bike with UV Hypersense 77 GHz radar — blindspot detection, lane-change & overtake alerts, rear collision warning.
  • X-47 Built for rough roads: 200 mm ground clearance & MRF Revz-AT adventure tyres vs 160 mm on the F77s.
  • X-47 Strongest pull — 610 Nm rear-wheel torque and quickest 0-60 (2.7 s), but capped at 145 km/h.
  • F77 Faster top end — 155 km/h for both Mach 2 & Superstreet.
  • Mach 2 vs Superstreet: mechanically identical — the split is stance only (race-bred vs street-oriented) plus colour options.
  • All Share powertrain, frame, brakes, 5" TFT + Linux OS, and the 7.1 / 10.3 kWh battery choice.
